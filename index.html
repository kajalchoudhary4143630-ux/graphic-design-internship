Logo concept

A house ("haus") built from a coral roof, a cream body, a sun for the creative spark and a teal wave for the vibe. The logo animates in four steps: roof draws, house rises, sun pops, wave flows. Exported as a video intro in After Effects, this same sequence works as a 3-second sting.
